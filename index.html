Synthetic-Data Fruit Detection for Simulated Robotic Grasping

A YOLOv8m detector trained entirely on procedurally generated PyBullet scenes finds a target fruit among others and guides a simulated Franka Panda arm to grasp and lift it.

Pipeline

Two parts work together: PyBullet builds the world, renders the camera view and drives the robot, while YOLOv8m does all the visual perception.

01
Generate scenes
Random orange, pear and banana layouts on a table
02
Auto-label
Bounding boxes taken from segmentation masks, no hand labeling
03
Train YOLOv8m
COCO-pretrained, fine-tuned on 3 fruit classes
04
Detect target
Find the requested fruit in a new RGB view
05
Localize in 3D
Box center plus depth buffer gives the grasp point
06
Grasp and lift
Panda arm approaches, closes and lifts the fruit

mAP@50 is saturated for every class, so mAP@50–95 is the more telling number. It shows banana boxes are slightly less tight, which matters for depth-based grasp-point estimates.

Target fruitGround-truth positionDepth-estimated position

The depth-estimated runs aim the arm only from the camera's view instead of reading the fruit's true position from the simulator. They matched the ground-truth runs, with median localization errors of 0.2–0.4 cm.

Key findings

What made the difference

  • Class imbalance. Early training data had 46 orange boxes against 559 banana boxes. Diagnosing and fixing this was the first step to reliable detection.
  • Seeing the scene. A detection-only diagnostic showed the detector was correct whenever the target was actually visible. Failures came from the scene, not the CNN.
  • Camera and robot setup. Re-aiming the camera and moving the robot base out of the table took banana success from 1 in 50 (v13) to 46 in 50 (v14).

Limitations and future work

  • Fruit are simple geometric primitives. Next step: realistic fruit meshes and photo textures.
  • Pear grasping is the weakest at 54%. Shape-aware grasp planning could help.
  • Everything runs in simulation. Testing sim-to-real transfer with real fruit images and a physical arm is the long-term goal.
  • This work is planned as the base for my M.S. in AI capstone.
